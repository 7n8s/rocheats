    ROCHEATS — DISCORD FAQ

    Answers to common questions about orders and support.

    03 / FAQ
    01

    How long does delivery take?

    • Card: Instant delivery.
    • Crypto: Usually instant, but may take 30–60 minutes to process.
    02

    Haven’t received your license key?

    • Check your spam/junk folder.
    • Check your RoCheats dashboard.
    • If it hasn’t arrived within 15–30 minutes, open a support ticket.
    03

    What payment methods are accepted?

    • Credit/debit cards.
    • Apple Pay and Google Pay.
    • Bitcoin, Litecoin, Ethereum, and other supported cryptocurrencies.
    04

    Do you offer refunds?

    • All sales are generally final.
    • Refunds may be considered for verified technical issues that cannot be resolved within 7 business days.
    05

    How do I download an executor?

    • Use the dropdown menu below and select the executor you’re looking for.
    • You can also open a support ticket and have a staff member provide you with direct links.
    06

    How long do unpaid orders last?

    • Unpaid orders are automatically canceled after 2 hours.
    • If you’ve paid but your order was canceled, contact support.
    07

    Having technical issues?

    • Head over to our discord server to get technical support.
    • Describe the issue and include screenshots if possible.
    08

    How do I contact support?

    • Head over to our discord server to open a support ticket.
    • A staff member will assist you as soon as possible.
